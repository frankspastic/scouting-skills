# Pack 97 — Austin, TX

Reference document compiled from [pack97.com](https://pack97.com/) on 2026-07-07.

## About

Cub Scout Pack 97 is a Cub Scout pack of the **Capitol Area Council** in Austin, TX. It was originally founded in **2002** by families from **Holy Family Catholic School**; in **2024** the charter organization changed to the Capitol Area Council. The pack still meets at Holy Family and maintains a strong Catholic identity, but membership is **open to any interested scout — any school or faith, boys and girls** — in kindergarten through fifth grade. The pack has graduated hundreds of Cub Scouts into the Scouts BSA program. It has about **50 youth members**.

**Mission statement:** Pack 97 delivers all aspects of the Cub Scout program to K-5 youth. The pack engages scouts in monthly Pack and Den meetings, 2 campouts per year, an overnight adventure, and a Pinewood Derby race to offer experience with outdoor skills, STEM, leadership, fitness, reverence, citizenship, crafting, and competing — all using the EDGE (Explain, Demonstrate, Guide, Experience) method in a safe environment. Scouts are inspired to "Do Your Best" (the Cub Scout Motto) while building lifelong friendships and having fun.

## Meetings

- **Pack meetings:** roughly once a month, Sunday afternoons, 1–2 hours. Usually in the Holy Family Catholic School gymnasium, occasionally at a local park.
- **Den meetings:** 1–2 per month per den (in addition to the pack meeting), about 1 hour each; schedule and location vary by den.
- Dens are grade-level groups of about 5–10 scouts; the pack keeps each grade in 1–2 dens.
- All activities follow **two-deep leadership** (2 registered adult leaders present); dens with girls require at least 1 registered female adult leader.

## How to Join

Short version:

1. Join Scouting America National: [online youth application](https://my.scouting.org/VES/OnlineReg/1.0.0/?tu=UF-MB-564paa0097) (also reachable via [Be A Scout](https://beascout.scouting.org/list/?zip=78717&program%5B%5D=pack&unitID=296555))
2. Pay [pack activity dues](https://pack97.com/dues-payment/)
3. Your den leader will reach out; if you don't hear anything within a week, use the [contact form](https://pack97.com/contact/)

Notes for prospective families:

- Visiting a few events before registering is welcome; registration gets more pressing around **October** (annual enrollment paperwork). Activities done before registering can't count toward advancements.
- Parents/guardians are not required to volunteer but are strongly encouraged to attend; for camping, a parent/guardian **must** attend with their scout.
- [New Member Interest Form](https://pack97.com/new-member-interest-form/) for families who want to be contacted.
- Optional [Pack Facebook group](https://www.facebook.com/groups/664258516917621).

## Uniforms

- **Field uniform** ("Class A"): the official shirt with patches and rank insignia. **Activity uniform** ("Class B"): optional matching t-shirt for den outings, campouts, and casual events (ordered through the pack website).
- **Pack 97 provides every member a neckerchief, slide, and handbook** — don't buy these.
- Pack numerals: **97**; den numeral: ask your den leader.
- Local purchase: [Steve Matthews Scout Shop](https://www.bsacac.org/about/shop-info/) (uniform sales often run in September). Buy big — starred items are reused for several years.
- **Lions (K):** Lion t-shirt (or navy Cub Scout shirt), navy shorts, Cub Scout belt; yellow neckerchief provided.
- **Tigers–Webelos (1st–4th):** navy Cub Scout shirt, navy shorts, Cub Scout belt. Neckerchief colors: Tiger orange, Wolf red, Bear light blue, Webelos plaid. (Webelos may switch to the tan/green uniform whenever the navy one is outgrown.)
- **Arrow of Light (5th):** tan Scout shirt, green (olive) shorts, green Scout belt, Webelos Colors (shoulder ribbons for adventure pins); plaid neckerchief provided.
- Hats and socks are optional for all ranks.

## Popcorn Fundraiser (Trails End)

- The annual fundraiser returns **70% of funds** to Pack 97 and the Capital Area Council; proceeds fund neckerchiefs, handbooks, belt loops, Pinewood Derby kits, rank patches, and more. **All scouts are expected to participate.**
- Dues earn-back program (introduced 2025): 49% of returned profits are refunded to the selling scout's family up to the amount of National + Pack dues; the other 51% goes to the pack.
- Register with Trails End: [app](https://qrco.de/trails-end) · [new account registration](https://www.trails-end.com/registration)
- Registration details: Council **Capital Area Council**, District **Chisholm Trail**, Unit **Pack 0097**, Unit Code **RTN6P**
- Pack 97 does **not** do wagon/door-to-door sales (no inventory on hand); sales are online/app-based. Helpers & Heroes donations (popcorn for military/first responders) also count.
- Typical season: selling starts in August; online sales end in late November. Check the [popcorn page](https://pack97.com/popcorn/) for the current year's dates and Parent & Scout Guide.
